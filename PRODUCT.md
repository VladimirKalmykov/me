## Brand Commitments
Confident, specific Russian and equivalent natural English. A dark-only, minimal, living interface with restrained semantic neon. After the hero, a pinned skills screen shows overall responsibility, then frontend, backend, infrastructure, AI and UI/UX through a native-scroll-scrubbed sequence; the truthful chronology follows. Real project periods, overlaps and gaps remain visible; scroll changes the active chapter and focused details reveal contribution and stack. Motion must explain information, not decorate it. Keep a compact real portrait and immediate name, role and contacts. No light website theme, theme toggle, image-generated static comps, decorative 3D, fake terminal chrome or endless identical cards. The current portrait is a placeholder; a new one is optional. PDFs remain separate light reading documents.
