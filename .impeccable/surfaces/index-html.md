# Portfolio surface

Mode: Experience, with recruiter scanability as a hard requirement.
Targets: general RU/EN portfolio and targeted frontend/fullstack RU/EN pages.

## Direction contract
THESIS: An engineering career is a truthful chronology of problems, decisions and impact, not a list of employers or a field of decorative effects.
OWN-WORLD: Dark-only minimalist interface with restrained semantic neon. Actual calendar intervals and gaps determine the visual structure. Small real portrait, immediate name, role and contact. Local Cyrillic type, SVG/CSS explanatory graphics and no image-generated static comps. PDFs have a separate light editorial system.
STORY: Identity and contact in the first viewport; scroll advances through large chapters from newest to oldest; a persistent time axis shows the active period and offers direct links. Technical cases deepen each chapter.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Name, role, core statement, contact and compact portrait; a decade ruler indicates that the page unfolds in time. No light-theme switch.
FORM: The user selected chronology on 2026-10-10. Code-led prototype in `prototype/chronology/` precedes production implementation. Map of decisions and system cutaway are deferred in `docs/feature-features.md`.
FINISH: Production routes require responsive, keyboard, no-JS, reduced-motion, factual and PDF review. The current prototype establishes style and behavior only.

## Evidence and constraints
Use PRODUCT.md for facts. Keep the 2010 commercial start separate from detailed chronology beginning December 2016. Do not infer causation from extension-count growth at YouGile. Keep gaps and overlaps truthful.
